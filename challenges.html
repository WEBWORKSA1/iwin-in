Official rules (summary)

  • Free entry. No purchase, payment or subscription is required, and paying doesn't improve your chances.
  • Skill-based. Winners are chosen by a panel against published criteria: relevance (30%), clarity (30%), originality (25%) and presentation (15%). There is no random draw.
  • Eligibility. Residents of India. Entrants under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. iwin.in staff and judges can't enter.
  • Originality. Entries must be your own and not previously published. AI tools may assist, but you must disclose how they were used.
  • Rights. You keep copyright. You grant iwin.in a non-exclusive licence to publish your entry with credit.
  • Prizes. Prize details are published before a challenge opens and can't be exchanged. Tax on prizes is handled as required by Indian law; see our prize tax guide.
  • Results. Announced on the winners page and by email.
