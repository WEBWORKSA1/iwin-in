Watch & learn

Short talks we recommend before you apply, with notes on how each one helps.

Grit: The power of passion and perseverance

Angela Lee Duckworth, TED. Why perseverance over years predicts achievement better than talent alone.

The power of believing that you can improve

Carol Dweck, TED. The growth mindset: treating 'not yet' as a step, not a verdict. Useful after any rejection.

Inside the mind of a master procrastinator

Tim Urban, TED. A funny, honest look at deadlines. Watch it before your next application window.

How great leaders inspire action

Simon Sinek, TED. Start with why. The same structure makes scholarship essays and hackathon pitches persuasive.

Turn watching into doing

Pick one idea from a talk and apply it this week: set a deadline in the planner, draft the "why" paragraph of an essay, or enter one weekly coding contest. Small, repeated attempts are how most winners start.
