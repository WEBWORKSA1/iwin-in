Quiz contest tips

  • Read every option before answering. Quiz setters love near-miss distractors.
  • Learn the reason behind each answer; the explanation is often the next question.
  • Follow current affairs for the 3–6 months before a contest.
  • In timed rounds, skip and return rather than freezing on one question.
