Government contests

Official contests and schemes run by Indian ministries and agencies. Logo designs, essays, quizzes, innovation awards and grants.

Entering MyGov contests well

MyGov regularly runs logo, tagline, essay, video and quiz contests for ministries. Each has its own rules page with eligibility, format and the date range. Log in with your MyGov account, read the brief and submit before the closing date. Winners are announced on the same platform.

Keep it original. Most government contests ask for entries that haven't been published or used elsewhere and transfer rights in the winning entry to the organiser. If that matters to you, read the intellectual property clause before submitting.
