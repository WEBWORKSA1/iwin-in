A 30-day preparation plan for quiz competitions

A week-by-week plan to prepare for general knowledge, business and current affairs quiz contests.

By the iwin.in editorial team. Updated 16 October 2026.

Quiz contests reward people who remember connections, not just facts. With 30 days and around 45 minutes a day, you can improve your scores noticeably. This plan suits general knowledge, current affairs and campus business quizzes.

Week 1: find your gaps

  • Take three practice quizzes, such as our daily GK quiz, and note every question you got wrong by topic.
  • Group mistakes into areas: Indian history, geography, science, sport, business, current affairs, arts.
  • Choose your two weakest areas to focus on.

Week 2: build the base

  • Spend 20 minutes a day on a weak area using a good reference book or reliable websites.
  • Spend 15 minutes on current affairs from a trustworthy newspaper.
  • Write every new fact as a question on a flashcard. Writing questions trains you to think like a quizmaster.

Week 3: learn connections

Good quiz questions link ideas: a company name's origin, a scientist and the prize named after them, a place and the event that made it famous. Each day, take five facts and ask "what is this connected to?" Look up one connection for each.

Week 4: practise under pressure

  • Do timed quizzes every day. Practise skipping and returning.
  • Practise with a partner: one reads, the other answers aloud. This matches buzzer and stage rounds.
  • Review flashcards from weeks 2 and 3. Spaced revision locks in memory.

On the day

  • Read or listen to the full question before answering; the last clause often changes the answer.
  • When unsure, reason out loud with your team. Eliminate impossible options first.
  • Don't dwell on a missed question. The next one is worth just as much.
