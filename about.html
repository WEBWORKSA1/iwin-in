About iwin.in

Good opportunities shouldn't depend on who you know.

Every year, scholarships go unclaimed and contests close with few entries from small towns, while students who could have won never hear about them. Information is scattered across ministry portals, college notice boards, WhatsApp forwards and hundreds of organiser websites, and mixed in with scams that charge "registration fees".

iwin.in brings the genuine, free-to-enter programmes together in one place. We link only to official organiser pages, explain how each kind of opportunity works and publish guides that help you apply well. We also run our own skill-based iwin challenges so more people can build a track record.

What we stand for

  • Free to enter, always. We don't list lotteries, betting, fantasy games or anything with an entry fee.
  • Official sources. Every listing links to the organiser's own website.
  • Useful over flashy. Guides are written to help you act, not to fill pages.
  • Clear about money. Ads and sponsorships keep the site free, and sponsored content is labelled.

How iwin.in is funded

We show ads from Google AdSense, offer labelled sponsorships and featured listings, and accept voluntary support from readers. None of these affects which genuine opportunities we list.

The name

"iwin" simply reads as "I win", the feeling we want every reader to have. iwin.in is independent and isn't connected with any company that uses "iWin" as a brand. See our trademark notice.
