The science and maths olympiad pathway in India, explained

How India's olympiad selection works, from the first national exam to international teams, and how to prepare at each stage.

By the iwin.in editorial team. Updated 16 October 2026.

India selects its teams for the International Science and Mathematics Olympiads through a multi-stage process coordinated by the Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (HBCSE). Understanding the stages helps you plan preparation realistically.

The stages at a glance

  1. Stage 1: national-level written exam. For the sciences, the first stage is a set of National Standard Examinations conducted with the Indian Association of Physics Teachers. For mathematics, the first stage is the Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics. Thousands of students take these each year.
  2. Stage 2: Indian National Olympiad. Top scorers from stage 1 take a harder national olympiad in their subject, with longer, proof-style or problem-solving questions.
  3. Stage 3: orientation-cum-selection camp. The best performers attend a camp at HBCSE with lectures, theory and, in sciences, experimental tests.
  4. Stage 4: pre-departure training. The selected team trains before travelling.
  5. Stage 5: the international olympiad. India's team competes with students from dozens of countries.

Exact eligibility, dates and fees for stage 1 change each year, so always confirm on the official HBCSE olympiad website.

How to prepare

  • Past papers first. Official past papers and solutions show the style and difficulty better than any guidebook.
  • Depth over breadth. Olympiad problems go beyond the school syllabus in reasoning, not just topics. Spend time on fewer problems and understand them fully.
  • Write full solutions. Later stages mark the quality of reasoning. Practise writing clear, complete answers.
  • Find a group. Students preparing together solve more and stay motivated.

Private olympiads vs the official pathway

Many private organisations run school olympiads with their own medals and rankings. They can be good practice, but they're separate from the official HBCSE pathway that selects India's international teams. Check which one you're entering before you register.

What if you don't qualify?

Olympiad preparation builds problem-solving skills that help in board exams, entrance tests and research programmes. Students who reach stage 2 often go on to science fairs such as the IRIS National Fair, or research programmes. Browse science and creative contests for more.
